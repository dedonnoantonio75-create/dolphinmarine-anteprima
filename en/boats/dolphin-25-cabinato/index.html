Dolphin 25 Cabin

When the day trip turns into a weekend

  • 750 cm Length
  • 252 cm Beam
  • 300 hp Max power
  • 8 People
  • C CE category

The same hull as the other 7.50 m boats, but everything changes below deck: a double berth forward, a separate marine toilet, a galley with sink, and wooden lockers built by hand in our own shed. This is the boat for people who do not want to be forced home by nightfall.
