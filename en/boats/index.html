The questions we get asked most

What is the difference between open, walk around and cabin?

The open has a completely clear cockpit and suits anglers or people who go out for the day with a crowd. The walk around adds side decks so you can walk round the cabin top, and a small cabin below. The cabin version has a proper cabin with a berth, heads and galley: you can sleep aboard.

Are the boats CE certified?

Yes. The 7.50 m boats are certified in design category B or C depending on the model, for the number of people shown on each spec sheet. The Dolphin 21 Open falls into the Italian "natante" class.

Can I fit any engine I like?

You can choose the make and the power up to the hull certification limit shown on the spec sheet. Inboard or outboard: we prepare the transom and the hydraulic box accordingly.

Do the photos show boats that have already been sold?

Many of them do: they are boats built here and delivered to real customers, photographed in the yard before launch. Yours will not be identical — and that is the point.
