The questions we get asked most

How do I get to the yard?

Melpignano is on the road between Maglie and Otranto. From the Lecce–Maglie dual carriageway take the Melpignano exit and follow the Asse Spina industrial estate: the yard is at number 13, with the boat yard visible from the road.

Do I need an appointment?

It is not compulsory, but a call first saves you arriving while we are laminating, which is the one job we cannot stop.

How quickly do you answer emails?

The same day on weekdays. If you are in a hurry, WhatsApp is faster.
